Design and implement a visually engaging “What we do” section under the Activities page that showcases the different divisions of Bocconi Students for Venture Capital.

Instead of a basic grid, create a modern, premium-looking card layout that fits seamlessly with the site’s dark, high-end aesthetic.

Structure
	•	Section label: Activities
	•	Main heading: What we do
	•	Below the heading, display 4 divisions:
	•	Business Development
	•	Media
	•	Venture Capital
	•	Articles

Content presentation

Each division should be presented as a card that includes:
	•	A minimal icon (yellow accent, consistent with brand)
	•	A clear title
	•	A short descriptive paragraph (use the provided content)

Design direction
	•	Match the site’s dark, VC-style aesthetic (black background, white text, yellow accents)
	•	Cards should feel elevated and premium, not flat:
	•	Subtle glass or soft dark background
	•	Rounded corners
	•	Light border or glow
	•	Soft shadow for depth
	•	Icons should sit slightly above or overlap the card for a more dynamic feel

Layout
	•	Use a 2x2 grid on desktop
	•	Stack vertically on mobile
	•	Maintain generous spacing and clean alignment

Interactions & animation
	•	Add scroll-triggered reveal animations:
	•	Cards appear with fade + upward motion + slight scale-in
	•	Stagger the entrance for a smooth sequence
	•	Add hover interactions:
	•	Card slightly lifts or scales
	•	Border or glow becomes more prominent
	•	Icon can subtly animate (scale or brightness)

Visual enhancements
	•	Consider subtle background elements (e.g. faint gradients, noise, or patterns) to avoid a flat look
	•	Keep animations refined and not distracting
	•	Ensure strong contrast and readability

Constraints
	•	Do not change the content itself
	•	Keep the design consistent with the rest of the landing page
	•	Ensure full responsiveness and smooth performance

Overall intent

This section should feel like a clear, structured breakdown of what the organization actually does, while maintaining a polished, modern, and slightly dynamic presence that reinforces credibility and professionalism.
