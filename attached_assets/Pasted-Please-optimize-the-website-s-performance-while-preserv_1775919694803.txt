Please optimize the website’s performance while preserving the current visual aesthetic as much as possible.

Goal

Reduce lag and improve smoothness, especially from the continuously moving background and multiple animated sections, without noticeably downgrading the design.

Priorities
	•	Keep the overall look and feel the same
	•	Preserve the dark premium aesthetic, motion language, and immersive background
	•	Focus on performance-minded refinement, not redesign

What to optimize
	•	Reduce the rendering cost of the moving background
	•	lower particle/dot density slightly if needed
	•	simplify any overly expensive continuous animations
	•	avoid unnecessary layered effects if they are expensive
	•	keep the same visual impression, just make it lighter
	•	Make animations more efficient
	•	prefer transform and opacity animations over costly layout or paint-heavy properties
	•	avoid animating large blur, shadow, mask, or filter effects continuously
	•	add GPU-friendly hints where appropriate, such as will-change: transform
	•	Reduce concurrent animation load
	•	keep scroll reveals and hover effects, but make sure they are lightweight
	•	avoid too many simultaneous observers or animation triggers running at once
	•	keep motion smooth and subtle rather than constantly heavy
	•	Optimize the hero / animated background loading strategy
	•	lazy-load or defer heavy visual layers if possible
	•	avoid blocking initial render with expensive visual effects
	•	use a lighter fallback if needed for weaker devices
	•	Improve responsiveness across devices
	•	use a lighter animation version on mobile or lower-performance environments
	•	respect prefers-reduced-motion with a toned-down variant rather than removing the aesthetic entirely

Constraints
	•	Do not redesign the page
	•	Do not remove the moving background entirely unless absolutely necessary
	•	Do not flatten the visual identity
	•	Keep the final result looking polished, premium, and visually close to the current version

Desired outcome

The website should feel:
	•	noticeably smoother
	•	less laggy in preview and production
	•	visually almost identical to the current design
	•	still immersive and high-end, just more efficient under the hood

When making tradeoffs, prioritize changes that deliver the biggest performance improvement with the smallest visible aesthetic change.
