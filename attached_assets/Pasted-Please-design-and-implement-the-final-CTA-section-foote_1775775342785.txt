1) Final CTA section

	•	soft gradient glow

Layout
	•	Center all content horizontally
	•	Use generous vertical spacing to give the section weight
	•	Ensure a strong visual hierarchy:
	•	prominent headline
	•	slightly softer supporting text
	•	clear, focused CTA button

CTA button
	•	Style the button to match the site’s design system
	•	Use a yellow-accent or dark premium button style
	•	Include subtle hover interactions (e.g. glow, lift, or brightness shift)
	•	Keep it elegant and refined

Motion
